Long car ride? Turn silence into a game.

A sleeping owl listens through the phone's microphone. As long as everyone stays quiet, it keeps snoozing. If someone makes a noise… it wakes up, a sad trombone plays and whoever made the noise is out!

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• Together against the clock: with no names, the whole team tries to stay silent until time runs out.
• Elimination: add two or more players. When the owl hears a noise, the app asks "Who was it?", says out loud who is out and everyone else keeps playing. The last one silent wins.

MADE FOR THE CAR
• Before each round the owl measures the background noise (engine, road) so it doesn't wake up because of it.
• Three hearing levels: Good for the car, Sharp for home and Super sharp for a quiet room.
• Games of 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes.
• The screen stays on while you play.
• A silence record to beat on your next trip.
• "Nobody, keep playing" for false alarms.

PRIVACY
• Only the microphone volume level is used, on the phone itself. No audio is recorded or saved.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics.
• Works offline.

In English and Spanish, following the device language.
